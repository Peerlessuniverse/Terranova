## 3. Points, rewards and counters
Preserve base formula: verified eligible diverted kg × weights organic 1, paper 2, plastic 3, metal/glass 4, correct B3 treatment 8; residual-to-material conversion ×6 **instead of** ordinary credit on same kg; mission points +0.1 verified BHI contribution (if metric implemented) minus untreated emissions/residue penalties. For other families proposed weights textile/wood/rubber 2, clean construction 1, licensed electronics safe treatment 8 (do not double classify B3), qualifying residual energy 1 subject to review; no points for kg simply purchased or transferred. Fractional points stored milli-points, floor at display. Daily diminishing return per source proposed: first 1000 base pts 100%, next 4000 50%, beyond 5000 20%; F4+ caps 5× after QA. Penalized points not capped. No double credit from intermediate recipes, industrial resale or repeated events. All points tied to final disposition receipt; separate mission bonus permitted but bounded. BHI point term disabled until formula validated. Karma numeric formula remains baseline; proposed delta normalized by handled kg over rolling 7 days: +20 × untreated emission burden ratio +10 × uncontrolled leakage ratio +5 × landfill ratio −5 × verified recovery ratio per active day, clamp 0..100; zero denominator no delta; this is game parameter not environmental science. B3 safe treatment credit separate from recycling rate. Keep season totals and lifetime stats separate; never count virtual kg as real kg.
